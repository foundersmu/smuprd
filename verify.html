What this page is

This is a contact-and-verification page. It routes you to the coordinating exercise staff so you can confirm, in real time, whether a specific individual is participating and what they are authorized to do. It is not a substitute for that confirmation, and it does not itself grant or prove any authorization.

How to verify

  1. Call the Exercise Control Cell at the number above.
  2. Cite the exercise number shown above and describe who and what you are inquiring about.
  3. Request confirmation of the individual's participation and the scope of their authorized activity. The staff can confirm the current roster.
