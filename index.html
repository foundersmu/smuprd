noun /ˌyo͞o-bi-kwə-təs ˈtek-ni-kəl sər-ˈvā-ləns/
01

The persistent ability to observe, detect, identify, locate, or infer activity through the increasingly pervasive technical systems surrounding modern operations.

Why SMU

Small, practitioner-led, and deliberate about fit.

SMU was founded in 2020 to translate signature reduction concepts developed inside complex operational environments into applied field instruction.

The organization is practitioner-led, operationally grounded, and deliberately small. We engage where timing, alignment, and readiness converge, and we tailor each engagement to a specific mission profile rather than running a fixed catalogue.

We work directly with the people who will use what we teach, adapting engagements to the mission.
